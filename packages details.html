6-Day Luxury Safari Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Maun

Arrival in Maun

Upon arrival at Maun International Airport, you'll be greeted by our team and transferred to your luxury lodge. Rest and prepare for your safari adventure.

Day 2-3: Okavango Delta

Premier Wildlife Experiences

Experience guided mokoro excursions through pristine waterways, afternoon game drives, and evening star-gazing at your luxury lodge. Spot elephants, buffalo, and big cats.

Day 4-5: Moremi Game Reserve

Game Drives & Nature Walks

Explore one of Africa's finest game reserves. Early morning and evening game drives with expert guides, bush walks, and relaxation at your lodge.

Day 6: Departure

Journey Home

Final breakfast at your lodge, transfer back to Maun airport for your onward journey.

Luxury Accommodations

Stay in some of Botswana's finest and most exclusive safari lodges, carefully selected for their exceptional service and prime locations.

🏨 Delta Camp

Located in the Okavango Delta with direct water access. Features elegant suites with private plunge pools, fine dining, and world-class service.

🏨 Moremi Lodge

Positioned in the heart of Moremi Game Reserve. Experience luxury tented suites, gourmet cuisine, and wildlife viewing from your private deck.

🏨 Chobe Safari Resort

Overlooks the Chobe River with stunning sunset views. Includes premium rooms, spa facilities, and boat cruises along the river.
